Hi there! We've got some great new features for you in this update.

• Real-time responses and reasoning in chats with tools, without waiting for the entire response to finish.
• Smoother, more reliable long responses and tool use.
• Provisional text from failed tool calls is no longer saved as an answer in your conversation history.
• Ask the chat to edit a current or earlier conversation image using your selected compatible image model.
• Select multiple images from your Photo Library at once, keeping them in the order you choose.
• Attach conversation images again without saving them outside the app.
• See thumbnails and preview your attached images before sending a message.
• Minor bug fixes and improvements for a smoother experience.

Thanks for your continued support and for helping us build the best possible LLM client together.

Remember: you can suggest and vote on new features from the Feedback section in Settings.
